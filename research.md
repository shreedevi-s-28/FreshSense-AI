# FreshSense AI - Research Notes

## Project Goal

Develop a neural network that predicts the freshness of fruits and vegetables using environmental storage conditions and observable quality indicators.

The dataset will be synthetically generated using storage recommendations and spoilage trends derived from published food science literature.


## Supported Produce

Fruits
- Apple
- Banana
- Orange
- Mango
- Grapes
- Tomato

Vegetables
- Cucumber
- Carrot
- Spinach
- Potato
- Onion
- Bell Pepper
- Broccoli
- Lettuce
- Cauliflower

---

## Common Features

- Produce Type
- Storage Temperature (°C)
- Relative Humidity (%)
- Storage Duration (Days)
- Firmness Score (0–10)
- Color Score (0–10)
- Smell Score (0–10)
- Visible Mold (%)
- Surface Damage (%)
